# Reverie Gear

Reverie Gear 为寂静装备（Silent Gear）添加 41 种武器与工具、8 套护甲，以及寂静装备工坊。

---

## 中文

### 新增内容

**武器与工具（41 种）**

- 刀剑与长柄：焰形剑、巨剑、天杀星刀、飞剑、远古之矛、长槊、突进长矛、战戟
- 重武器：湮灭者、雷霆战锤、焚灭者、锻锤、绞肉机、潮汐利爪
- Malum 联动：灵魂镰刀、奥法镰刀、挽魂锋镰、提尔锋、咒术法杖、混沌四侵权杖、咒界重斧、侵蚀权杖、裂界锚刃
- 远程：自动装填弩、诅咒之弓、追踪弓、回旋镖、鞭子、悠悠球、泵动充能霰弹枪、虚空突击肩炮
- 法术：法杖、法术书
- 工具：广域采掘镐、多功能镐、旋刃、拐杖
- 防御：壁垒
- 其他：furioso、引雷针

**护甲（8 套）**

重型、法袍、恶念要塞、灵魂猎手、召唤师、武僧、游侠、旅人。

**寂静装备工坊**

包含蓝图槽、部件列表、材料槽、成品预览和制作按钮的工作台。

## English

Reverie Gear adds 41 weapons and tools, 8 armor sets, and the Gear Forge workstation to Silent Gear.

### What it adds

**Weapons and tools (41)**

- Blades and polearms: Flamberge, Greatsword, Tiansha Star Blade, Flying Sword, Ancient Spear, Astrape, Lunge Spear, Soul Spear
- Heavy weapons: Annihilator, Brontes, Incinerator, Infernal Forge, Meat Shredder, Tidal Claws
- Malum: Soul Scythe, Arcane Scythe, Soulreaper Scythe, Tyrving, Hex Staff, Unwinding Chaos Staff, Weight of Worlds, Erosion Scepter, Sundering Anchor
- Ranged: Autoloader Crossbow, Cursed Bow, Seeking Bow, Boomerang, Whip, Yo-yo, Pump-Charge Shotgun, Void Assault Shoulder Weapon
- Magic: Staff, Spellbook
- Tools: Wide Excavator, Multi-Function Pickaxe, Gale Spinner, Cane
- Defense: Bulwark
- Other: furioso, Lightning Spike

**Armor (8 sets)**

Heavy, Robe, Malignant Stronghold, Soul Hunter, Summoner, Monk, Ranger and Traveler.

**Gear Forge**

A workstation with a blueprint slot, part list, material slots, result preview and craft button.

### Optional mods

Install the mod and the following gear is registered and available.

| Installed mod | Available gear |
|---|---|
| **Malum** | Soul Scythe, Arcane Scythe, Soulreaper Scythe, Tyrving, Hex Staff, Unwinding Chaos Staff, Weight of Worlds, Erosion Scepter, Sundering Anchor, Malignant Stronghold armor, Soul Hunter armor |
| **Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks** | Staff, Spellbook, Autoloader Crossbow, Tiansha Star Blade, Lightning Spike, Robe, furioso |
| **L_Ender's Cataclysm** | Infernal Forge, Soul Spear, Void Assault Shoulder Weapon, Cursed Bow, Seeking Bow, Incinerator, Brontes, Ancient Spear, Astrape, Annihilator, Meat Shredder, Tidal Claws, Bulwark |
| **Terra Entity** | Boomerang, Whip, Yo-yo, Flying Sword |
| **Hazen's Stuff** | Summoner, Monk, Ranger and Traveler armor |
| **Nether's Expanse** | Pump-Charge Shotgun |
| **Lodestone** | Cane |

Additional requirements: the Spellbook needs Curios; furioso also needs Malum; the four Hazen's Stuff armor sets need GeckoLib and Iron's Spells, and Ranger armor also needs Apothic Attributes.

### Requirements

- Minecraft 1.21.1
- NeoForge 21.1.x
- Silent Gear 4.2.0+
- Silent Lib 10.3.0+
